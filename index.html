Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about air duct cleaning in Austin

How often should I get my air ducts cleaned?

Every 3-5 years for most homes. If you have pets, allergies, or recent renovations, every 2-3 years is recommended.

How long does air duct cleaning take?

Most residential air duct cleanings take 2-4 hours depending on the size of the home and the condition of the ducts.

Will air duct cleaning reduce my energy bills?

Yes. Clean ducts improve airflow efficiency, which can reduce energy costs by 10-30% in some cases.

Do you clean dryer vents too?

Yes. We clean the full dryer vent run from dryer to exterior to prevent fire hazards and improve drying efficiency.

Is air duct cleaning messy?

No. We protect your home and clean up after every job. No mess left behind.
