2. Complete EVERY field: name, address (or service area), phone, website, hours, categories
3. Primary category: "Air Duct Cleaning Service" (or "HVAC Contractor")
4. Secondary categories: "Air Conditioning Repair Service", "Duct Cleaning Service", "Indoor Air Quality Service"
5. Add 10+ photos (job photos, team, equipment, before/after)
6. Post weekly updates (tips, promotions, recent jobs)
7. Respond to ALL reviews (positive and negative)
8. Add Q&A section with common questions
9. Enable messaging
10. Add service list with descriptions

## Review Strategy
- Ask every customer for a review after service
- Send follow-up text/email with direct review link
- Target: 50+ reviews with 4.8+ rating
- Respond to all reviews within 24 hours
- Encourage photo reviews (before/after)
- Once you have 10+ reviews, uncomment the AggregateRating schema in index.html
